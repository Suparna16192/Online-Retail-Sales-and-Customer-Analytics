# Online Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing online retail transactions to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product returns, and geographical revenue distribution using Power BI.

The objective is to transform transactional data into actionable business insights that can support revenue growth, customer retention, and operational improvements.

**Tools & Technologies:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling, Data Visualization

**Dataset Period:** December 2009 – December 2011

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Identify high-performing countries and products.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns and repeat purchases.
- Monitor product returns and identify potential areas of improvement.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for business growth.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which countries contribute the highest revenue?
2. How does revenue change across months and quarters?
3. Which products experience the highest return volumes?
4. What proportion of customers make repeat purchases?
5. Which customer segments contribute most to revenue?
6. Are returns concentrated in specific products or geographical markets?

## Business Recommendations

The analysis can support the following business initiatives:

- **Revenue Optimization:** Prioritize high-performing markets and investigate declining revenue trends.
- **Product Quality Improvement:** Investigate products with unusually high return rates and review quality-control processes.
- **Customer Retention:** Introduce loyalty programs and targeted campaigns to encourage repeat purchases.
- **Market Performance:** Investigate geographical differences in revenue and return rates.
- **Sales Optimization:** Use purchasing trends to identify opportunities for cross-selling and increasing average order value.

*Recommendations should be finalized using validated findings from the dataset.*
